## What WhatsApp does to your files

WhatsApp handles the same file completely differently depending on how you attach it:

| How you send it | What happens | Max size |
|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| **As media** (gallery/camera picker) | Compressed. Videos are re-encoded to roughly 480p | Small |
| **As media with HD toggle** | Compressed less. Videos go at up to 720p | Small |
| **As a document** | Sent untouched, original quality | **2 GB** |
| **As a link** | No WhatsApp limits at all | Unlimited |

That compression is why a crisp 4K clip arrives looking like it was filmed through a window. The good news: two of the four rows above keep your file intact.

## Method 1: Send It as a Document (Full Quality, up to 2 GB)

The document path bypasses compression entirely, and it accepts photos and videos, not just PDFs:

1. Open the chat and tap the **attach** icon (paperclip on Android, **+** on iPhone).
2. Choose **Document**, not Photos or Gallery.
3. Browse to the photo or video file and send it.

The recipient gets the exact original, bit for bit, up to 2 GB per file. It arrives as a file rather than an inline preview, which is a small price for full quality.

<Tip>
On iPhone, media lives in the Photos app, which the document picker doesn't show directly. Save the video to the **Files** app first (Share → Save to Files), then attach it as a document from there.
</Tip>

## Method 2: The HD Toggle (Better, Still Not Original)

When you select a photo or video in the media picker, tap the **HD** button at the top before sending. You can also make HD the default under **Settings → Storage and data → Media upload quality**.

HD media is noticeably better than standard (video goes up to 720p instead of roughly 480p), but it is still compressed. Use it for everyday sharing; use the document method when the file itself matters.

## FAQ

**Why do my WhatsApp videos look blurry?**
Videos sent as media are re-encoded at roughly 480p (720p with the HD toggle) to save data. Send the video as a document instead, and it arrives in original quality.

**What's the maximum file size on WhatsApp?**
2 GB per file, sent as a document. There's no paid tier that raises it. Bigger files need a link.

**How do I send a 5 GB video on WhatsApp?**
Paste a [Transfer.zip](https://transfer.zip) link in the chat. Quick Transfer has no size limit and streams the file directly to the recipient's browser.

**Does sending as a document work for photos too?**
Yes. A photo sent as a document keeps its full resolution and metadata, which is exactly what you want for printing or editing.

**Is a transfer link as private as WhatsApp?**
With Transfer.zip, yes in the way that matters: files are end-to-end encrypted, and the decryption key stays in the link fragment, which never reaches any server. Only people with the link can open the transfer.
