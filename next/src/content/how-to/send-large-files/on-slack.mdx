## Slack's file limits, including the surprise

Slack allows up to **1 GB per file** and **10 files per message**. Unlike most tools, the limit is identical on every plan: upgrading to Pro or Business+ buys longer message history, not bigger uploads.

Free workspaces have a second problem that bites later:

<Warning>
On the free plan, messages and files older than **90 days** are hidden from view, and content older than **one year is permanently deleted**. That design file you dropped in #general in March is on a countdown.
</Warning>

So there are really two reasons to reach for a link: files over 1 GB *can't* be uploaded, and on free workspaces, files under 1 GB quietly expire.

## Method 2: Use a Cloud Drive Your Team Already Pays For

If your company runs on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, upload to Drive or OneDrive and paste that link. Slack unfurls it, permissions follow your workspace rules, and it costs nothing extra.

The friction is the usual one: access requests. A link that opens instantly for you may throw a permission wall for the contractor in the channel. For mixed audiences (freelancers, clients, agencies), a transfer link that just downloads is kinder.

## FAQ

**What's the maximum file size on Slack?**
1 GB per file, up to 10 files per message, on all plans.

**Does Slack Pro increase the upload limit?**
No. Paid plans extend message history and add features, but the 1 GB per-file cap stays. This surprises a lot of teams mid-upload.

**What happens to files on a free Slack workspace?**
They're visible for 90 days. After that they're hidden, and once they're older than a year, Slack deletes them permanently.

**How do I send a whole project folder in Slack?**
Either zip it and upload (if under 1 GB), or send it through a transfer link, which preserves the folder structure and has no size cap.

**Is it safe to share work files through an external link?**
Transfer.zip Quick Transfers are end-to-end encrypted, so only people with the link can decrypt the files, a stronger guarantee than a public cloud-drive link. For long-lived links, set an expiry that matches how long the file should be available.
