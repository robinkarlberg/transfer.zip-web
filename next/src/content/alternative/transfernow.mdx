<TLDR>
TransferNow free = **5 GB** per transfer, **7-day** retention, max **3 recipients**. Fair, but beatable.

The top alternative is **Transfer.zip**: no size limit at all on free real-time transfers, and paid plans with 1 TB transfers and 365-day retention starting at **$6/month**.
</TLDR>

## Why look beyond TransferNow?

TransferNow is one of the more honest services in this space: a usable free tier with password protection, EU hosting, no dark patterns. The limits are simply modest. Free transfers cap at **5 GB** with **3 recipients** and 7-day availability, and the Premium plan's 250 GB ceiling sits below what heavy senders eventually need. If your files or your audience outgrew it, here's where to look.

---

## 1. Transfer.zip - No caps on free, more for less on paid

![A screenshot of Transfer.zip homepage.](/img/content/services/transfer-zip.png)

The free **Quick Transfer** mode removes the number TransferNow's free tier is built around: there is no size limit, because files stream directly between browsers, end-to-end encrypted, instead of being stored. Send 500 MB or 500 GB, as many times a day as you like.

Paid plans (from **$6/month** billed yearly, with a 7-day free trial) add stored transfers: 200 GB on Starter, **1 TB** on Pro with **365-day** retention, download tracking, custom branding, and a custom domain.

<Pro>Open source</Pro>
</GoodFor>

## 2. SwissTransfer - Ten times the free allowance

![A screenshot of the SwissTransfer landing page.](/img/content/services/swisstransfer.png)

SwissTransfer does what TransferNow's free tier does, but at **50 GB** per transfer instead of 5, with expiry up to 30 days, passwords, and download limits. Run by Swiss host Infomaniak, no account, no ads. It has no paid path at all, so business users pass through rather than settle in.

<Pro>Up to 30-day expiry</Pro>
<Pro>Passwords and download limits included</Pro>
<Pro>Swiss hosting and privacy law</Pro>
</GoodFor>
<BadFor label="Cons:">
<Con>No paid tier, no upgrade path</Con>
<Con>No branding or analytics</Con>
</BadFor>

## 3. Smash - Unlimited free size, if you can wait

![A screenshot of the Smash landing page.](/img/content/services/smash.png)

Smash removes the free size cap entirely, with the known catch: transfers over **2 GB** enter a non-priority queue at peak times. Retention is 7 days free. Pro at **$10/month** buys priority, 250 GB, 30-day retention, and customization. Good for occasional big sends where timing is flexible.

<GoodFor label="Pros:">
<Pro>No hard size limit on free</Pro>
<Pro>Clean, ad-free interface</Pro>
<Pro>Customization on Pro</Pro>
</GoodFor>
<BadFor label="Cons:">
<Con>Queue over 2 GB on free</Con>
<Con>Retention tops out at 30 days on Pro</Con>
<Con>Closed source</Con>
</BadFor>

## 4. Filemail - Same category, business trimmings

![A screenshot of the FileMail landing page.](/img/content/services/filemail.png)

Filemail's free tier is stingier than TransferNow's (5 GB but only **2 transfers per day**), yet the paid side reaches further: Pro (~$12/month) does 250 GB with UDP-accelerated speeds, and Business plans lift the size limit entirely. Consider it when raw upload speed across continents or compliance checkboxes are the actual requirement.

</GoodFor>
<BadFor label="Cons:">
<Con>Free tier limited to 2 transfers per day</Con>
<Con>More expensive at every tier</Con>
</BadFor>

## 5. WeTransfer - The household name, with new strings

WeTransfer remains the name clients recognize, but the free tier now requires an **account**, allows **10 transfers per rolling 30 days** at up to **3 GB**, and links expire after **3 days**. The 2025 terms-of-service episode (an ML-training clause, later walked back) also dented its privacy reputation. Fine for the occasional small send; the details of the [full comparison are here](/comparison/wetransfer).
