<TLDR>
Filemail free = **5 GB** per transfer, **2 transfers per day**, files deleted after **7 days**. Pro is around **$12/month** for 250 GB, and truly unlimited sizes require the pricier Business plan.

The top alternative is **Transfer.zip**: unlimited free real-time transfers, plus 1 TB stored transfers with 365-day retention for less money.
</TLDR>

## Why look beyond Filemail?

Filemail is a capable service with a business focus: UDP-accelerated uploads, resumable transfers, compliance features. The friction is in the packaging. The free tier's **2 transfers per 24 hours** rule bites the moment you forget an attachment, retention is 7 days, and file size upgrades are stair-stepped behind plans: around $12/month (Pro) for 250 GB, with unlimited sizes reserved for Business at roughly $15-18 per user per month.

If you don't need the enterprise features, you can get bigger transfers for less.

## 1. Transfer.zip - Unlimited transfers, longer retention, lower price

![A screenshot of Transfer.zip homepage.](/img/content/services/transfer-zip.png)

Transfer.zip's free **Quick Transfer** has no size limit and no daily counter: files stream directly between browsers, end-to-end encrypted, as often as you like. Paid plans (from **$6/month** billed yearly, 7-day free trial) add stored transfers up to **1 TB** with retention up to **365 days**, view and download tracking, and custom branding on the Pro plan.

Against Filemail's ladder, the comparison is straightforward: longer retention than Pro (365 vs 30 days), bigger transfers than Pro (1 TB vs 250 GB), at roughly half the monthly price.

## 3. Smash - No size limit on free, with a queue

![A screenshot of the Smash landing page.](/img/content/services/smash.png)

Smash's free tier has no hard size limit, though transfers over **2 GB** wait in a non-priority queue at busy times, and files last 7 days. Pro at **$10/month** brings 250 GB priority transfers and 30-day retention: essentially Filemail Pro's numbers for slightly less money, with a nicer interface and without the 2-per-day free limitation.

Premium reaches **250 GB** per transfer with **365-day** availability for less than Filemail Pro, and the Team plan covers 10 users flat instead of per-seat pricing.

## 5. MASV - If you genuinely need unlimited, giant files

Filemail Business's headline feature is unlimited file size. If that's the part you actually use, MASV is the specialist: single files up to **15 TB**, built for media production, billed at **$0.25/GB** pay-as-you-go with no subscription required. For occasional monster deliveries, paying per gigabyte beats a monthly business plan; for constant volume, it gets expensive fast (see our [MASV alternatives guide](/alternative/masv)).
