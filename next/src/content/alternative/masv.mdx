MASV bills **$0.25/GB** every time a file leaves its cloud. One 100 GB delivery = **$25**. A weekly one = **$100+/month**.

For regular large deliveries, a flat-rate plan wins: **Transfer.zip Pro** moves up to **1 TB per transfer** for **$12.50/month** billed yearly, with unlimited transfers.

## Why look beyond MASV?

MASV is built for professional media delivery and it's good at it: single files up to 15 TB, no seat fees, reliable accelerated transfers. The business model is the thing to examine. Pay-as-you-go sounds fair until you do the arithmetic on regular work: at **$0.25/GB**, delivering 100 GB of footage costs $25, and doing that weekly costs more per month than most unlimited-transfer subscriptions. Storage past the free 5 days bills separately at $0.07/GB/month.

The free tier covers 15 GB per month, then the meter runs. If your deliveries are frequent rather than occasional, flat-rate services do the same job for a fraction of the cost.

## 2. Filemail Business - Unlimited file sizes on a subscription

Filemail's Business plan (around $15-18 per user per month) removes the file size limit entirely and adds UDP acceleration, which matters for intercontinental uploads. It's the closest subscription match for "MASV but flat rate," priced accordingly.

## When MASV is still the right call

Fairness where due: if you deliver a 5 TB feature master twice a year, MASV's pay-as-you-go beats every subscription on this page, and nothing else here touches its 15 TB single-file ceiling or its dedicated media-pipeline integrations. MASV is priced for the occasional enormous delivery. It's the *recurring* deliveries where the meter hurts.
