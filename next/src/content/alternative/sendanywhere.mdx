## Why look beyond Send Anywhere?

Send Anywhere earned its user base with a great idea: pick files, get a 6-digit key, type it on the other device, done. The service still works, but the free tier has tightened over the years. Share links cap at **10 GB** and expire after **48 hours**, and since 2023 free accounts draw from a **10 GB per month** download allowance on links. Ads sit in the free apps, and the old PLUS subscription was discontinued, replaced by an "Email Add-on" plan (Lite around $5.99/month) that mostly buys back monthly download data.

Meanwhile the company behind it (Estmob, acquired by Rakuten) has pointed its energy at Rakuten Drive, a separate cloud product. If you're sending big files regularly, there are better homes for the workflow.

## 1. Transfer.zip - The 6-digit code experience, without the caps

![A screenshot of Transfer.zip homepage.](/img/content/services/transfer-zip.png)

Transfer.zip's **Quick Transfer** works the way Send Anywhere fans expect: select files, get a **6-digit code** (or QR code, or link), enter it on the receiving device, and the transfer starts immediately, device to device. The differences are in what's missing: the size limit, the monthly download allowance, the ads, and the account requirement are all gone.

It's also **end-to-end encrypted**, which Send Anywhere's link transfers are not, and the whole platform is [open source](https://github.com/robinkarlberg/transfer.zip-web). For stored links that outlive the moment, paid plans go up to 1 TB per transfer with retention up to 365 days.

## 2. SwissTransfer - Bigger free links that actually stick around

![A screenshot of the SwissTransfer landing page.](/img/content/services/swisstransfer.png)

Where Send Anywhere gives you 10 GB for 48 hours, SwissTransfer gives you **50 GB** with links that live up to **30 days**, plus optional passwords and download limits. Free, no account, no ads, run by Swiss host Infomaniak. There's no code-pairing workflow and no paid tier, but for pure link sharing it's the most generous free option around.

<GoodFor label="Pros:">
<Pro>50 GB per transfer, free</Pro>
<Pro>Links last up to 30 days, not 48 hours</Pro>
<Pro>Password protection included</Pro>
<Pro>No ads, no account</Pro>
</GoodFor>
<BadFor label="Cons:">
<Con>No device-to-device code pairing</Con>
<Con>No paid tier for bigger needs</Con>
<Con>Upload-then-download, so it's slower than direct transfer</Con>
</BadFor>

## 3. TransferNow - Solid link transfers with free passwords

![A screenshot of the TransferNow landing page.](/img/content/services/transfernow.png)

TransferNow's free tier does **5 GB** per transfer with 7-day retention, up to 3 recipients, and password protection included. Paid plans stretch to 250 GB (Premium) or 500 GB (Team) with availability up to 365 days. EU-hosted with antivirus scanning.

## 4. LocalSend - Device-to-device, no internet at all

If what you liked about Send Anywhere was moving files between your own devices, LocalSend does it with no server involved whatsoever: open-source apps for every major platform that transfer directly over your local network, even with the internet down. The trade-off is installing the app everywhere and staying on the same network.

<GoodFor label="Pros:">
<Pro>Fully offline, local network only</Pro>
<Pro>Free and open source, no ads</Pro>
<Pro>Apps for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS</Pro>
</GoodFor>
<BadFor label="Cons:">
<Con>Both devices need the app installed</Con>
<Con>Same network required</Con>
<Con>No links for sending to other people</Con>
</BadFor>

## 5. Rakuten Drive - The official successor, if you want storage

Rakuten built Rakuten Drive on Send Anywhere's transfer technology, and it shows where the company's attention went. It's a cloud storage product with file-sending on top: free accounts can send sizable transfers, paid plans raise that to 50 GB per send with terabyte-class storage. If what you actually need is a cloud drive that sends files, this is the in-family upgrade. If you just want to move a file from A to B, it's more product than the job requires.

<GoodFor label="Pros:">

<Pro>Storage and transfer in one product</Pro>
<Pro>Backed by a large company</Pro>
</GoodFor>
<BadFor label="Cons:">
<Con>Cloud-drive workflow, not quick device-to-device</Con>
<Con>Requires an account</Con>
<Con>Transfer caps still apply per plan</Con>

### Choosing the Right Send Anywhere Alternative

**If you need:**

1. **The code-pairing workflow without the caps** → Transfer.zip, the only one here that keeps the 6-digit code and drops the limits
2. **Free links that last** → SwissTransfer's 50 GB for 30 days beats 10 GB for 48 hours
3. **Passwords on a free tier** → TransferNow or SwissTransfer
4. **Moving files between your own devices at home** → LocalSend, fully offline
5. **A cloud drive from the same lineage** → Rakuten Drive

**Why Transfer.zip stands out:**
- Keeps the one thing Send Anywhere got right (type a code, transfer starts) and removes everything else: caps, expiry windows, download allowances, ads
- End-to-end encryption that link-based competitors don't offer
- Open source, with a real upgrade path to 1 TB stored transfers when you need links that persist
