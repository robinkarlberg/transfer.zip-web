<TLDR>
On free tiers this isn't close: **SwissTransfer** does **50 GB** per transfer with links up to 30 days, passwords included, no account. **WeTransfer** free does **3 GB**, 10 transfers per 30 days, 3-day links, account required.

WeTransfer's case is the paid path and the brand. SwissTransfer has **no paid tier at all**.

## David and the household name

WeTransfer built the category and owns its mindshare. SwissTransfer, run by Swiss hosting company Infomaniak, is the quiet counterexample: a genuinely free service with limits most paid plans don't match. Comparing them is really comparing two questions: how much do you send, and does anyone pay?

---

## The free tiers, side by side

| | SwissTransfer | WeTransfer Free |
|---|---|---|
| **Max per transfer** | **50 GB** | 3 GB |
| **Transfers per month** | Up to 500/day | 10 per rolling 30 days |
| **Link expiry** | Up to 30 days | 3 days |
| **Password protection** | Yes | No |
| **Download limits** | Yes, configurable | No |
| **Account required** | No | **Yes**, since late 2024 |
| **Ads** | None | Yes, on download pages |

The free comparison ends about as one-sided as tables get. A 4K video project that WeTransfer free rejects outright uploads to SwissTransfer sixteen times over, with a password, for a month.

WeTransfer's remaining free-tier advantages are softer: recipients know the interface, and the apps and polish are a notch above.

---

## Where WeTransfer actually wins

**A paid path exists.** When you outgrow free limits, WeTransfer sells Starter ($6.99/mo, bigger transfers, same 10-per-month ration and 3-day expiry) and Ultimate (~$25/mo, unlimited 1 TB transfers, custom expiry, passwords, branding). SwissTransfer sells nothing: 50 GB and 30 days is where the road ends, forever. Business users who need branding, tracking, or longer retention can't stay.

**Recipient familiarity.** For client-facing work, "here's a WeTransfer" requires no explanation anywhere on earth.

---

## Where SwissTransfer wins

**Every number that matters on free.** Sixteen times the size, ten times the expiry, passwords and download limits that WeTransfer reserves for its $25 tier.

**Privacy posture.** Files sit in Infomaniak's Swiss data centers under Swiss privacy law, with no ad trackers on the pages. WeTransfer spent July 2025 walking back terms-of-service language about machine learning after a creative-community revolt; no files were reportedly misused, but the episode is part of the record now.

**No account, no ration.** Sending doesn't require signing in, and there's no monthly counter to manage.

---

## What neither offers

- **Real-time transfer.** Both are upload-then-download services: your 50 GB goes up, waits, then comes down. There's no direct device-to-device mode for when both parties are online.
- **End-to-end encryption.** Both encrypt in transit and at rest, and both hold the keys.
- **Retention past 30 days** without WeTransfer's top tier. SwissTransfer stops at 30 days flat; WeTransfer's custom expiry starts at ~$25/mo.
