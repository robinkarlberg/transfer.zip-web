<TLDR>
**Dropbox Transfer**'s limits depend entirely on which Dropbox plan you already pay for: **2 GB** free, **50 GB** on Plus, **100 GB** on Standard and up.

**WeTransfer** free does 3 GB with 10 transfers per 30 days and 3-day expiry; its $25 Ultimate tier does 1 TB.

Rule of thumb: already paying for Dropbox? Use Transfer. Otherwise WeTransfer, or better, a standalone service without either's strings.
</TLDR>

## Two different products wearing the same hat

WeTransfer is a file-sending company. Dropbox Transfer is a file-sending *feature*, bolted onto a storage subscription. That difference explains almost everything in this comparison: WeTransfer's limits follow its own price ladder, while Dropbox Transfer's limits follow whatever Dropbox plan you happen to have.

---

## Size limits and expiry, per plan

| | Max per transfer | Expiry | Password |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dropbox Basic (free)** | 2 GB | 7 days, fixed | No |
| **Dropbox Plus / Family** | 50 GB | 7 days, fixed | No |
| **Dropbox Standard and up** | 100 GB | Custom (default 30 days) | Yes |
| **Dropbox Business Plus / Enterprise** | 250 GB | Custom | Yes |
| **WeTransfer Free** | 3 GB, 10 per 30 days | 3 days | No |
| **WeTransfer Starter ($6.99/mo)** | 300 GB per rolling 30 days | 3 days | No |
| **WeTransfer Ultimate (~$25/mo)** | 1 TB | Custom | Yes |

Patterns worth noticing:

- **Consumer Dropbox plans get rigid 7-day expiry and no passwords.** Even Plus subscribers paying for 2 TB of storage can't change when a Transfer link dies. Custom expiry and passwords start at the work-tier plans.
- **WeTransfer's cheap tier keeps the annoying parts.** Starter lifts the size cap dramatically but keeps 3-day expiry and the 10-transfer ration.
- **Recipients need no account on either service**, which is why both beat "just share a Drive/Dropbox folder link" for one-off delivery.

---

## Where Dropbox Transfer wins

**It's already included.** If your team pays for Dropbox Standard, you have 100 GB transfers with custom expiry and passwords at no extra cost. Adding a WeTransfer subscription on top would be paying twice.

**Cleaner separation from your storage.** A Transfer is a snapshot: recipients get a copy, not access to your live folder, and download counts are tracked.

## Where WeTransfer wins

**It exists apart from a storage plan.** You can pay $6.99 and send 300 GB without adopting an ecosystem.

**Bigger ceilings at the top.** Ultimate's 1 TB per transfer beats anything Dropbox Transfer offers on any plan.

**Recipient familiarity.** The WeTransfer download page is the most recognized in the category, for what that's worth in client work.

Marks against both: WeTransfer's July 2025 terms-of-service stumble (machine-learning language, retracted after backlash) rattled its core creative audience. Dropbox Transfer's limits-by-plan matrix means most consumer users are stuck with fixed 7-day, password-less links.

---

## Who should pick what

- **Already on a paid Dropbox work plan** → Dropbox Transfer, it's free money.
- **On free Dropbox or a consumer plan** → not Dropbox Transfer; 2-50 GB with fixed 7-day expiry and no password is weak. WeTransfer free (3 GB) or a standalone service.
- **Sending 100 GB+ regularly without a Dropbox contract** → WeTransfer Ultimate, or read on.
